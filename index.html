04 / A FEW SWEET DETAILS

Good to know.

When is pickup or delivery available? +

Thursday and Friday after 4:30 PM, and Saturday 10 AM–6 PM. All requests must be confirmed by Carmen. Scheduling rules can be edited in config.js and in the backend.

How does the limited weekly drop work? +

Each flavor starts with 12. When live inventory is connected, a checkout reserves treats, and each Sunday at 11:59 PM Eastern the unsold balance rolls forward with 12 more. A paid order is never added back automatically.

What ingredients do the cookies contain? +

The shared ingredient list includes flour, sugar, brown sugar, vanilla, Oreos, Biscoff, graham crackers, chocolate chips, and marshmallows. Exact recipe and allergen information varies by flavor and must be checked with Carmen.

Are the cookies allergy-friendly? +

No allergy-free claim is made. Possible allergens include wheat, dairy, egg, and soy. These are made in a shared home kitchen. Always confirm with Carmen before purchasing if you have allergies.

How do I pay? +

Connected checkout redirects to Square's secure payment page. Availability of Apple Pay and other methods depends on the buyer's device and Carmen's Square settings. For Zelle or another method, text Carmen at 463-867-2562.
